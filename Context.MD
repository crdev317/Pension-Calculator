# Domain Glossary

## Contribution
The amount the user pays into their Pension pot each month, before Tax relief is added.
Not an employer contribution and not a one-off lump sum; the calculator models neither.

## Pension pot
The money saved in the user's pension, invested until Retirement age. The user enters its current value.
It belongs to a defined contribution pension. Not a final salary (defined benefit) pension and not the State Pension; the calculator includes neither.

## Tax relief
The basic-rate tax relief added to each Contribution: for every £80 the user pays, £20 is added, so £100 goes into the Pension pot.
Not higher- or additional-rate relief, which is claimed through a tax return and never reaches the Pension pot.

## Current age
The user's age today, in whole years. Together with Retirement age, it sets how many years the Pension pot grows for.

## Retirement age
The age at which the user plans to start taking money from their Pension pot. The user enters it, and the Projected pot is the Pension pot's estimated value at this age.
Not the State Pension age, and not an Assumption.

## Assumption
A value the projection depends on that is not a fact about the user: Growth rate, Charges or Inflation. Every Assumption is shown alongside the Projected pot.
Not a user input such as Current age, Retirement age, Contribution or the current value of the Pension pot.

## Charges
The yearly cost of running the pension, expressed as a percentage of the Pension pot's value and taken from it. An Assumption.

## Growth rate
The assumed yearly rate at which the investments in the Pension pot grow, before Charges and before Inflation.
An Assumption, not a prediction or a guarantee.

## Inflation
The assumed yearly rise in prices, used to express the Projected pot in Today's money. An Assumption.

## Estimate
What every result the calculator shows is: an illustration based on the user's inputs and the Assumptions.
Not financial advice and not a guarantee.

## Projected pot
The Estimate of the Pension pot's value at Retirement age, shown in Today's money.
Not tax-free cash and not a retirement income; the calculator shows neither.

## Today's money
A value adjusted for Inflation so that it reflects what it would buy at today's prices.
The Projected pot is always shown in Today's money, not as the larger cash figure the Pension pot would hold at Retirement age.
